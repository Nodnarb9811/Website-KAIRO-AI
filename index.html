Embed. Diagnose. Build. Operate.

Four moves. Decisive at the front. Compounding at the back.

Embed
I come inside your business for a week. Not a survey. Not a workshop. I sit with your team and watch the work happen.
Diagnose
I map the moments — the five places in your operation where AI compresses weeks into minutes, hours into seconds, and expensive humans into expensive humans doing better work.
Build
I ship the system. Agents, automations, voice, vision, internal tools — whatever the moment demands. Production-grade. Yours.
Operate
I stay. AI doesn't sit still — models evolve, edge cases emerge, new moments open up. I tune the system as it runs, fold in each new capability as it arrives, and keep the work compounding. The operator who built it is the operator who keeps it sharp.
Built once. Operated always.
